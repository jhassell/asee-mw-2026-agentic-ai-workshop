# Adapt one line

Six minutes. You leave with one change to one course, written down.

On your exit card, line 4, write three things:

1. **The course.** Name it.
2. **One line you will add to one assignment.** Pick one, or write your
   own:
   - "Your demonstration must include a rollback story: something that
     went wrong and how you recovered."
   - "Keep a delegation log: which assistant you used, the key prompts, one
     mistake it made, and how you caught it."
   - "Every prompt edit gets a changelog entry: what changed, what you
     expected, what you observed. 'Made it better' is not an entry."
   - "Every source you cite must be quoted, and the quote must be findable
     in the source."
3. **One rubric criterion that grades that line, with a weight.** Example:
   "Rollback story is specific and recoverable: 10 points."

Then swap cards with your neighbor and ask one question: *how would a
student game this?* Fix your line if they found a way.

The full versions of these lines, with the grading levels behind them, are
in [`starter-assignment.md`](starter-assignment.md) and
[`rubric-template.md`](rubric-template.md).
